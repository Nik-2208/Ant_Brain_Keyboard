## 🧬 Biological Inspiration & Scientific Positioning

**ANT BRAIN** is a biologically informed computational model inspired by ant nervous systems and collective colony behavior.

It is **not** a reconstruction of a real biological ant brain, biological connectome, or complete neural circuit. Biological concepts are used as inspiration for computational abstractions, simulations, and engineering experiments. Where exact biological evidence is unavailable, the implementation is described as a computational or modelled abstraction rather than established biological fact.

## 📚 Scientific Inspiration & References

The algorithms and abstractions in ANT BRAIN are informed by scientific research across neurobiology, stigmergy, and multi-agent systems:

### Biological & Neuroscience References
- **Insect Neuropil Architecture**: Informed by insect central complex, antennal lobe, and mushroom body organization studies (*Heisenberg, M., 2003*).
- **Spiking Neural Network Dynamics**: Informed by Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) formulations (*Lapicque, L., 1907*; *Gerstner & Kistler, 2002*).

### Ant Colony & Stigmergy References
- **Stigmergic Coordination & Pheromone Decay**: Inspired by Pierre-Paul Grassé's theory of stigmergy (*Grassé, P.-P., 1959*) and Marco Dorigo's Ant Colony Optimization (*Dorigo et al., 1996*).
- **Decentralized Division of Labor**: Informed by task-threshold models of division of labor in social insects (*Bonabeau, Theraulaz, & Deneubourg, 1996*).

### Machine Learning & Multi-Agent Systems References
- **Decentralized Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning**: Informed by spatial multi-agent coordination literature (*Tan, M., 1993*; *Busoniu et al., 2008*).

*Note: Cited research informed the conceptual models used in ANT BRAIN. Cited researchers did not create or contribute directly to the ANT BRAIN codebase.*
